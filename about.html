1950 – 2023

Founder of Picture Palace

IN LOVING MEMORY

Mr. Farhat Yusufi

"Bhaijaan"

Every great legacy begins with a dream. In 1950, Mr. Farhat Yusufi opened a small photography shop named Picture Palace. What started with limited resources soon became a place known for honesty, dedication and exceptional craftsmanship.

At a time when photography was still finding its place in this region, he emerged as one of the pioneering photographers of Western Uttar Pradesh. His work inspired trust among families and respect throughout the photography community.

To the people of Moradabad he was never just a photographer. He was lovingly known as "Bhaijaan". His studio became a place where knowledge was shared freely, young photographers were encouraged and lifelong friendships were built.

Many of today's respected photographers across Moradabad and nearby districts proudly consider themselves his students. His greatest achievement was not only the beautiful photographs he created, but also the generations of photographers he inspired.

Although he left us in 2023, his values continue to guide every frame we capture. His vision lives in every wedding, every family portrait, every smile, and every memory preserved through Picture Palace Productions.

OUR JOURNEY

FROM THE NEWSROOM
TO OUR ROOTS

FASAHAT YUSUFI

News Anchor • Deputy Channel Head

Every Story Has A Beginning.
Mine Led Me Back Home.

After completing my education in Mass Communication, I moved to Delhi to pursue a career in television journalism.

Over the years, I had the privilege of working with leading news channels as a News Anchor and later as a Deputy Channel Head. The newsroom taught me discipline, truth, responsibility and the art of telling stories that matter.

Yet no matter how far life takes us, our roots always call us back.

In 2013, I returned to Moradabad to continue the dream established by Mr. Farhat Yusufi.

Together with my brothers Sabahat Yusufi and Arafat Yusufi we transformed Picture Palace into Picture Palace Productions, combining timeless values with modern cinematic storytelling.

"I once reported stories. Today, I preserve them forever."
